Why Group Travel?

The biggest reason: everyone lands together.

Booking as a coordinated group gives us one shared arrival and departure plan instead of trying to piece together dozens of unrelated reservations.

One Airport Pickup

AUS + PHX + SFO → MIA → STI together

Everyone joins the same Miami-to-Santiago flight and arrives at STI at 12:23 PM. No repeated airport runs, no group waiting hours for another flight, and no one arriving alone.

Price Stability

Save toward a known total instead of watching airfare change while you decide when to purchase.

Guaranteed Group Space

Your deposit holds a place in the group instead of risking the itinerary selling out later.

One Locked-In Group Rate

When people book separately, lower-priced seats can sell out and later travelers may see higher fares. Group booking helps keep the cost more predictable for everyone.

Better Planning

A solid flight headcount makes transportation, rooms, food, and excursions much easier to plan accurately.

Less Guest Stress

You don't need to research flights, coordinate connections, or figure out how to arrive with everyone else.

Comparable Individual Price

The American group fare is in line with the comparable individual itinerary while adding the benefits of coordinated group travel.

Why This Flight?

We compared the group options before choosing American.

We wanted the best balance of price, travel time, arrival and departure times, and the ability to keep travelers from all three departure cities coordinated.

A little context on the airfare

April is high season in the Dominican Republic.

Our trip falls during the country's dry-season tourism peak, when airfare is typically already elevated. We weren't expecting bargain-season prices; our goal was to find a reasonable itinerary that protected the group's timing and coordination without paying a major premium.

Why American won

Delta was similar in price, but the 12–14 hour itineraries would cost us most of Thursday and require an extremely early Sunday departure. JetBlue had a more reasonable travel time, but its $1,226.57 AUS quote was substantially higher and still could not coordinate PHX or SFO travelers. American was the only option that combined multi-city coordination, a 12:23 PM group arrival, practical Sunday flights, and competitive high-season pricing.

Delta and JetBlue comparison pricing/timing reflects the group-travel options we were quoted or researched for Austin travelers. Those programs did not provide the same multi-city group structure as American.
