Don’t Forget

The Essentials

If you forget a swimsuit, we can fix that. These are the things that are much harder to replace once you’re at the airport.

Passport Your most important item.
Wallet + Cards Bring more than one payment method if possible.
Prescription Medications Bring enough for the full trip, ideally in original packaging.
Phone We know nobody leaves the house without this, but figured it was worth mentioning. For travel info, WhatsApp, Uber, maps, and staying connected with the group.
Glasses / Contacts If applicable: including contacts, solution, and a backup pair (or two) if you have one.
House Keys Bring a physical key even if you normally use an electronic lock, we wouldn't want anyone sitting outside of their house at night because they came home to a power outage.

The Basics

Pack for warm, humid, tropical weather.

April around Sosúa and Cabarete is typically warm during the day, comfortable at night, humid, and occasionally showery.

Average High ~82°F ~28°C
Average Low ~72°F ~22°C
Humidity ~78% Warm + humid
Ocean ~79°F ~26°C

What that means for packing: Think lightweight and breathable. April is warm and humid, and brief tropical showers are possible, so a light rain layer is plenty — no need to pack for cool weather.

Lightweight clothes Swimsuits Sunglasses Sandals / flip-flops Light layer for evenings Compact rain layer / umbrella One nicer dinner outfit Small day bag
For the Flight

Make the travel day comfy.

A few things aren't essential, but you'll probably be glad you packed them before multiple hours of airports and planes.

Earbuds / Headphones Don't download three movies and then realize you have no way to listen to them.
Download Entertainment Save movies, shows, podcasts, books, playlists, or games before leaving Wi-Fi.
Portable Battery Helpful for long travel days, especially if airport outlets are occupied.
Charging Cable Keep one in your personal item instead of burying every charger in your luggage.
Light Layer The Dominican Republic will be warm. The airplane may have other ideas.
Empty Water Bottle Bring it through security empty and fill it once you're inside the terminal to save $5 and still be hydrated for all the shenanigans once we land!
