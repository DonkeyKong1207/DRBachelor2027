Booking a Ride

A few tips make private transportation much easier.

01
Message instead of calling

WhatsApp is the easiest way to communicate with many local drivers.

02
Send the essentials

Include your pickup location, destination, pickup time, and number of passengers.

03
Share your exact location

Use WhatsApp’s location-sharing feature if the driver needs precise GPS coordinates.

04
Mention larger groups

Ask for a passenger van or minibus if your group is too large for a standard car.

05
Agree on the price first

Confirm the total fare before the ride begins so there are no surprises at the destination.

Easy Copy-Paste Message

Hi! We need transportation for 4 people from [pickup] to [destination] at approximately 7:30 PM. Our location is attached. Are you available, and what would the total fare be?

Want to Go Local?

Guaguas + Motoconchos

You’ll see guaguas (shared minibuses) and motoconchos (motorcycle taxis) throughout the area. They’re a normal part of everyday transportation in the Dominican Republic and can be a fun way to experience getting around like a local.

For this trip, Uber or a pre-arranged private driver will generally be the easiest option—especially for groups, nighttime plans, or anyone who would rather not negotiate transportation.

However, if you’re comfortable negotiating, understand that pricing and routes may be less predictable, and want the local experience, you absolutely can try one of these classic options. Agree on the fare first, know where you’re headed, and use your judgment.

One extra note for motoconchos: motorcycle travel naturally carries more physical risk than riding in a car. Use a helmet and choose another option if you’ve been drinking.
