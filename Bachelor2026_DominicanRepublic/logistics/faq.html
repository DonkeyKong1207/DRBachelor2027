Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the villa really free?

Yes. The accommodation is fully covered. Guests will not be asked to split the cost of the villa.

Do I have to be in the wedding party to attend?

No. This trip includes wedding-party members and other close friends we want to celebrate with.

Do I have to attend every activity?

No. We'll make it clear which activities are major group plans and which are completely optional.

When should I book my flight?

We'll add recommended arrival and departure windows once the schedule is more finalized.

What airport should I use?

Puerto Plata's Gregorio Luperón International Airport (POP) is the primary recommended airport.

Can I bring a guest?

Please check with us before adding anyone who was not included in the original invitation so we can make sure lodging and transportation still work.
